Naruto Uzumaki

Who is Naruto Uzumaki?

Naruto Uzumaki is the main character of the anime series Naruto. He dreams of becoming Hokage.

His personality

  • Brave
  • Friendly
  • Stubborn
  • Helpful

His abilities

Naruto can use different ninja techniques.

His biggest dream

He wants to become Hokage and be respected by everyone in the Hidden Leaf Village.
