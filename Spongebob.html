Spongebob Squarepants

Who is Spongebob Squarepants?

SpongeBob is a yellow sea sponge. He lives in a pineapple under the sea.

His friends

  • Patrick
  • Squidward
  • Sandy
  • Mr. Krabs

His job

He works at the Krusty Krab and makes delicious Krabby Patties.

His hobbies

He loves jellyfishing and spending time with Patrick.
